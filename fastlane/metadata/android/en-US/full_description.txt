Speak, and Voice Notes does the rest. Record a note, import an audio file or share one from another app, and the app turns it into text in the language you spoke, with an optional English translation, a short title and a summary, all saved in a searchable notes library.

TRANSCRIBE ON YOUR PHONE OR WITH YOUR OWN AI KEY
• On this device: download a speech model once and transcribe with no key, no account and no internet. Choose Whisper for any language, Omnilingual for more than 1,600 languages, or IndicConformer models made for Hindi, Tamil, Telugu and Malayalam.
• Or add your own API key from Sarvam (built for Indian languages), OpenAI or Google Gemini. The app talks to that provider directly from your phone, and you pay them for what you use, at their rates. No subscription.
• Titles, summaries and templates use a text model from Sarvam, OpenAI, Gemini or OpenRouter, which offers free models.

WHAT YOU GET
• Record for as long as you need. Long recordings are transcribed in parts while you speak
• Live notes: pick a template while recording and its notes update every few minutes
• Templates turn a note into meeting minutes, a to-do list, a recipe, grammar review or speaking feedback, or write your own
• Speaker labels show who said what
• Tags, folders and search across all your notes
• A custom dictionary for names and terms, plus find and replace
• Import m4a, mp3, wav and other audio files, or share audio from other apps
• Play the original audio back, and copy any transcript or translation
• Back up your notes to a file and restore them on a new phone
• Retry a transcription if your connection drops. Your recording is saved first, so nothing is lost

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Notes and audio are stored on your device only
• Your API keys are kept in your phone's secure storage
• No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking
• With an on-device model, audio never leaves your phone. With an online provider, it goes only to the provider you choose, and only when you transcribe a note
• Optionally delete the audio once a note is transcribed

GOOD TO KNOW
• Offline models are one-time downloads, from about 140 MB to about 1 GB. Larger models are more accurate but slower and need more memory
• Online transcription needs an API key and an internet connection, and usage is billed by that provider
• Language support and accuracy depend on the model you pick

Voice Notes is a simple, no-frills tool for turning speech into searchable text, in the language you actually speak.
